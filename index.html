ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

HyperCities is a city made thick with time, maps, voices, and possible futures.

This is a static research prototype for experiencing HyperCities: Thick Mapping in the Digital Humanities through the historical Map Library and a source-grounded Hyperbook. It is neither a conventional academic site nor a recreation of the original GIS platform.

MAP
A dark world field holds the real extents of historical maps.
TIMEWELL
Choose a place to drill a temporal CORE: each map stays geographically proportioned while its date rises or descends through the well.
HYPERBOOK
Maps and actions may evoke exact book passages, concepts, people, events, and source pages—with provenance retained.
TAKE A MAP
A selected layer can travel outward as a live MapLibre example, a Google Earth Network Link, or a QGIS XYZ source.

A small grammar

Pan and zoom the field. Click or tap to core a place. Move up or down through time. Read the book fragment that enters. Take the named STRAY route when you want a source-grounded lateral turn; TRACE keeps the residue of that dérive. On touch screens, the floating arrows mirror these movements.

The site remains a simple GitHub Pages build: static files, MapLibre, deck.gl, and no backend.
